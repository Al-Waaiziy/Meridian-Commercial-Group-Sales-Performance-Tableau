# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

The **Meridian Commercial Group (MCG) Sales Performance & Business Intelligence Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project developed using Tableau.

The project focuses on examining sales performance across multiple business dimensions, including revenue generation, product category contributions, individual product performance, geographical distribution, salesperson contributions, and monthly revenue trends.

The dashboard transforms sales information into an organized visual reporting environment, allowing users to explore business performance, identify major revenue contributors, and examine differences across products, regions, and salespeople.

The project demonstrates the application of Tableau in developing interactive business intelligence dashboards that communicate analytical findings and support data-driven decision-making.

# 2. PROJECT OBJECTIVES

The main objective of this project is to develop an interactive Tableau dashboard for monitoring and evaluating sales performance.

Specific objectives include:

- Evaluate overall revenue performance.
- Examine total quantity sold.
- Analyze the total unit price metric presented in the dashboard.
- Identify product categories contributing to revenue.
- Determine the leading revenue-generating products.
- Compare revenue contributions across geographical regions.
- Evaluate individual salesperson performance.
- Examine monthly revenue patterns and fluctuations.
- Provide interactive filters for focused business analysis.
- Communicate business performance through clear data visualizations.

**Total Revenue – $280,355**

Represents the total revenue displayed in the dashboard.

**Total Quantity – 8,901**

**Total Unit Price – $5,132**

Represents the value displayed under the Total Unit Price indicator.

These metrics provide a high-level summary of the sales performance represented in the report.

# 6. DASHBOARD ANALYSIS

The Tableau dashboard is organized into five major analytical sections.

## 6.1 Revenue by Category

This section uses a packed-bubble chart to compare revenue contributions across product categories.

The categories visible in the dashboard include:

- Beverages
- Sauces
- Jams and Preserves
- Dairy Products
- Candy
- Canned Meat

The size of each bubble represents its relative revenue contribution.

This visualization provides a quick comparison of category performance and helps identify differences in revenue contributions across product groups.

## 6.2 Revenue by Product

This section uses a horizontal bar chart to compare revenue generated by the top seven products.

The dashboard displays the following products and revenue values:

| Product | Revenue |
|:---|---:|
| Coffee | $75K |
| Curry Sauce | $69K |
| Marmalade | $41K |
| Mozzarella | $33K |
| Crab Meat | $25K |
| Beer | $18K |
| Chocolate | $18K |

Coffee recorded the highest displayed product revenue, followed by Curry Sauce.

The visualization provides a clear comparison of leading product contributors.

## 6.3 Revenue by Region

A pie chart is used to examine revenue distribution across four geographical regions.

| Region | Revenue |
|:---|---:|
| North | $117K |
| South | $65K |
| West | $57K |
| East | $41K |

The North region recorded the highest displayed revenue contribution, while East recorded the lowest.

This analysis provides a geographical perspective on sales performance and highlights differences in regional revenue contributions.

## 6.4 Revenue by Salesperson

This section uses a horizontal bar chart to evaluate revenue generated by individual salespeople.

## 6.5 Monthly Revenue Trend

The monthly revenue trend uses an area chart to visualize revenue changes across the months of the year.

The visualization provides a time-based view of sales performance and helps examine:

- Monthly revenue fluctuations.
- Periods of stronger revenue activity.
- Periods of comparatively lower revenue.
- Changes in revenue throughout the year.

The area chart provides an overview of monthly performance and supports further investigation into sales trends.

# 7. INTERACTIVE DASHBOARD FEATURES

The dashboard includes four interactive filters designed to make the analysis more flexible.

### Product Name Filter

Allows users to select individual products and examine their contribution to sales performance.

The product revenue chart is configured to display the top seven products by revenue.

### Region Filter

Allows users to focus on specific geographical regions:

- East
- North
- South
- West

### Salesperson Filter

Enables users to explore revenue contributions associated with individual salespeople.

### Category Filter

Allows users to select product categories and examine their associated sales performance.

These interactive controls provide a more flexible reporting experience and allow users to investigate different dimensions of the business.

# 8. KEY FINDINGS AND BUSINESS INSIGHTS

Based on the figures displayed in the dashboard, the following observations can be made.

### Product Performance

Coffee recorded the highest displayed product revenue at approximately $75K, followed by Curry Sauce at approximately $69K.

Marmalade and Mozzarella followed with approximately $41K and $33K respectively.

This highlights the strong revenue contributions of Coffee and Curry Sauce among the products displayed.

### Regional Performance

The North region generated approximately $117K, making it the highest revenue-generating region in the dashboard.

South contributed approximately $65K, followed by West at $57K and East at $41K.

This shows differences in regional revenue contributions.

### Salesperson Performance

Nancy Freehafer recorded the highest displayed salesperson revenue at approximately $88K.

Anne Larsen followed with approximately $65K, while Jan Kotas recorded the lowest displayed contribution at approximately $9K.

The comparison highlights differences in individual revenue contributions.

### Overall Performance

The dashboard reports:

- Total Revenue: $280,355.
- Total Quantity: 8,901.
- Total Unit Price: $5,132.

Together, these indicators provide a summary of the business metrics presented in the Tableau report.

# 9. BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

The findings provide several areas for further business investigation.

### Product Strategy

Management could investigate the factors contributing to the strong revenue performance of Coffee and Curry Sauce and identify opportunities to strengthen other products.

### Regional Strategy

The North region's leading revenue contribution could be examined to identify factors that may support stronger performance in other geographical markets.

### Salesperson Performance

Individual revenue comparisons could support performance reviews, targeted coaching, and further investigation into differences in sales contributions.

### Revenue Monitoring

Monthly revenue patterns could help management monitor sales fluctuations and improve future planning.

These recommendations are potential business actions for further investigation rather than established explanations for the observed results.
